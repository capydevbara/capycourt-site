How a club night runs

Three taps from arrival to game on

Leave the tablet at the side of the court. Players check in, Capycourt does the thinking, and the organiser gets the night back.

1

Check in

Tap a name to check a player in. Add guests on the night, and let anyone sit out when they need a break.

2

Get fair games

Capycourt suggests balanced matches for every free court, so people get good games without anyone juggling names.

3

Keep courts moving

Start the game, record the result, and the next one is ready. Made a mistake? Undo, shuffle or swap players by hand.

Smarter than a queue

Fair games come from more than waiting time

Capycourt looks at everyone's skill rating, how many games they have played, how long they have waited and how often pairs have played together recently. Then it suggests the games that give the fairest night.

⚖

Balanced, fair matching

Suggestions weigh skill ratings, games played, waiting time and recent partners, so games are close and the same four don't keep meeting.

⏱

Nobody left waiting

People who have waited a while are guaranteed a place in an upcoming game, and a short break never costs anyone their turn.

🔄

Adapts as courts free up

When a court finishes, the next game is reworked to keep players rotating. Changes are highlighted so you can see what moved.

📈

Ratings that learn

Skill ratings update from real results, and new players are marked until they have played enough games to settle.

🎛

Your club, your rules

Choose how mixed games are, set the number of courts, and take a court out of use mid-session.

🔒

Your data, your device

Club data stays encrypted on your device. Optional automatic backup goes to your own Google Drive, locked with your passphrase.

Phone or tablet

Run the whole night from one tablet

Capycourt works on Android phones and tablets. A tablet at the side of the court shows every court, the waiting list and the next game at a glance.

  • Learn it in minutes. A built-in tutorial and a practice club let organisers try everything without touching real data.
  • Bring your members. Import members and ratings from a spreadsheet, and export them whenever you like.
  • Keep control. An organiser PIN protects settings, and members agree to a privacy notice in the app.

Questions

Good to know

Is Capycourt free?

Yes. The app is free, with no ads and no tracking.

Which sports does it work for?

Capycourt is built for social badminton club nights first. Any doubles racket-sport club that rotates players through courts can try it.

Do my members need to install anything?

No. The organiser runs the night from one phone or tablet. Members just turn up and check in.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Capycourt is on Android first, and an iPhone version is planned for later.

Where is my club's data stored?

On the organiser's device, encrypted. The organiser can also switch on automatic backup to their own Google Drive. Backups are locked with a passphrase that only they hold. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

How do I send feedback or report a problem?

Use the feedback page. It only takes a minute, and we read everything.
